# Glossary map

The words this repository has settled are kept in more than one glossary, because a word one plugin avoids another may need. Each glossary covers the paths named in backticks beside it, and a term belongs to the glossary covering the part whose concept it is.

- [Shared words](GLOSSARY.md): the words every plugin shares, such as the compatibility run, bb core and the thread tree. Covers `GLOSSARY.md`, `.github/`, `scripts/ci/` and `docs/explanation/how-the-plugins-fit-bb.md`.
- [Thread Glance](plugins/thread-glance/GLOSSARY.md): the shelves, cards and lifecycle of the sidebar thread list. Covers `plugins/thread-glance/`, `docs/explanation/thread-glance-shelves.md`, `docs/how-to/thread-glance-switch-sidebar.md`, `docs/reference/thread-glance-actions.md`, `docs/reference/thread-glance-list.md`, `docs/reference/thread-glance-settings.md` and `docs/tutorials/thread-glance-first-run.md`.
- [Thread Usage](plugins/thread-usage/GLOSSARY.md): the cost of a thread and its header chip. Covers `plugins/thread-usage/`, `docs/explanation/thread-usage-counting.md`, `docs/how-to/thread-usage-connect-litellm.md`, `docs/how-to/thread-usage-set-prices.md`, `docs/reference/thread-usage-agent-tool.md`, `docs/reference/thread-usage-cli.md`, `docs/reference/thread-usage-cost-sources.md`, `docs/reference/thread-usage-settings.md` and `docs/tutorials/thread-usage-first-run.md`.
- [Team Onboarding](plugins/team-onboarding/GLOSSARY.md): the checklist items, fixes, approvals and machine rules a team's manifest drives. Covers `plugins/team-onboarding/`, `docs/explanation/team-onboarding-checks.md`, `docs/how-to/team-onboarding-approve-commands.md`, `docs/how-to/team-onboarding-provision-manifest.md`, `docs/reference/team-onboarding-cli.md`, `docs/reference/team-onboarding-items.md`, `docs/reference/team-onboarding-manifest.md`, `docs/reference/team-onboarding-settings.md` and `docs/tutorials/team-onboarding-first-run.md`.
- [OpenAI-compatible inference](plugins/openai-inference/GLOSSARY.md): the endpoints bb offers as AI services and the AI tasks it hands them. Covers `plugins/openai-inference/`, `docs/explanation/openai-inference-requests.md`, `docs/how-to/openai-inference-local-server.md`, `docs/reference/openai-inference-settings.md` and `docs/tutorials/openai-inference-first-run.md`.
- [Cache Keeper](plugins/cache-keeper/GLOSSARY.md): compacting idle threads and keeping waiting threads warm before their prompt cache expires. Covers `plugins/cache-keeper/`, `docs/explanation/cache-keeper-timing.md`, `docs/how-to/cache-keeper-compact-a-thread.md`, `docs/reference/cache-keeper-cli.md`, `docs/reference/cache-keeper-settings.md` and `docs/tutorials/cache-keeper-first-run.md`.
- [UI Tweaks](plugins/ui-tweaks/GLOSSARY.md): the appearance settings bb lacks and the views they apply to. Covers `plugins/ui-tweaks/` and `docs/reference/ui-tweaks-settings.md`.
